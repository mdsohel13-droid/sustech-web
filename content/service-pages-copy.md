### FAQ
- **How do I know if my building needs lightning protection?** A risk assessment to IEC 62305-2 weighs your structure, location, contents and occupancy against the strike risk and tells you whether — and what class of — protection is required.
- **What earth-resistance value should we target?** It depends on the application (general installations, substations and sensitive equipment differ). We design to the appropriate target and test to verify it's met.
- **Can you test our existing earthing?** Yes — we measure earth resistance and soil resistivity and report whether your system meets standard, with remediation if not.
- **Is lightning/earthing protection needed for compliance or insurance?** Often, especially for industrial sites and tall structures. We document everything to standard so it satisfies compliance and insurer requirements.
- **Does this protect our electronics from surges?** Yes — coordinated SPDs protect connected equipment from transient over-voltages.

### FAQ
- **What are IR and ER testing, and why do we need them?** IR (insulation resistance) testing checks that cable and equipment insulation is sound; ER (earth resistance) testing verifies your earthing is effective. Together they catch faults that cause shocks, fires and downtime — and they're often required for compliance and insurance.
- **How often should we test?** It depends on your installation, regulations and insurer, but periodic testing (commonly annual, or to a set schedule) keeps you compliant and catches degradation early. We'll recommend an interval for your site.
- **Do you provide certificates for audits and insurers?** Yes — every job is handed over with a documented report showing measured values, pass/fail against standard, and recommended actions.
- **Can thermography find problems before failure?** Yes — infrared surveys reveal hot spots in panels, connections and equipment that signal a developing fault, so you can fix them on a planned basis instead of after a breakdown.
- **Can you test while we keep operating?** Thermography is non-intrusive and done live; some electrical tests need a planned outage. We schedule around your operations to minimise disruption.
