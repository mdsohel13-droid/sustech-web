## PART 1 — THE FOOLPROOF PLAN (read first)

### 1.1 Objective
Turn the chronic pain of **load-shedding + rising diesel cost** into demand for **Solar + LFP BESS**, and
route every interested person into one of three capture points: the **AI chatbot** (now answers in Bangla &
reads photos), **WhatsApp**, or a **phone call**. Secondary objective: announce the new website as proof that
Sustech is a modern, credible engineering partner.

### 1.2 Who we are talking to
| Segment | Where | Pain | The hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Factory / RMG / industrial owners & managers | LinkedIn + FB | Diesel cost, production stoppage on outage | "Solar + LFP BESS = 75% lower energy cost, <1s backup" |
| Commercial (offices, hospitals, cold storage, schools) | FB + LinkedIn | Outage downtime, UPS that dies in minutes | "Strategic energy independence, not a noisy generator" |
| Home / shop owners (premium) | Facebook | Heat, kids' study stops, food spoils | "HeroEE Light — silent, instant, solar-ready backup" |

### 1.3 Platform strategy
- **Facebook** → emotional, Bangla-first, vertical 4:5 images + 1 carousel + 1 short reel. Goal: reach, saves, DMs.
- **LinkedIn** → authority, English-first (Bangla summary), landscape/portrait infographics. Goal: credibility, leads.
- **One subject per day**, posted on **both** platforms with the tone adapted. Cross-post the website link every day.

### 1.4 The 3-day calendar (subject-wise)
| Day | Subject | Facebook | LinkedIn | Best post time (BST) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 1** | **The Problem + The Launch** (load-shedding pain → new AI website) | Post **1A** (launch/hero) | Post **1B** (the Diesel Trap) | FB 1:00–2:00 PM & 8:00–9:30 PM · LI 10:00–11:00 AM |
| **Day 2** | **The Solution** (Solar + LFP BESS done the right way) | Post **2A** (Diesel vs Lithium carousel) + **2C** (HeroEE for home/shop, evening) | Post **2B** (BESS as an investment / ROI) | FB 1:30 PM & 9:00 PM · LI 9:30 AM |
| **Day 3** | **Proof + Action** (real projects, certifications, free assessment) | Post **3A** (proof) + **3C** (CTA/lead-gen, evening) | Post **3B** (founder voice + CTA) | FB 1:00 PM & 8:30 PM · LI 10:30 AM |

Bangladesh engagement peaks: **lunch 1–2 PM** and **post-Isha 8–10 PM**. Avoid early morning. Keep the
evening slot for emotional/home posts (people feel outages most at night).

### 1.5 Engagement tactics (the part most people skip)
1. **Reply to every comment within 30–60 min** for the first 3 hours (Meta rewards early velocity).
2. **Pin a first comment** on each post with the website link + "Send us a photo of your electricity bill or
   panel in our chat — our AI will give you a tailored idea instantly."
3. **Ask one question** in every caption (comments = reach). e.g. "মাসে কত টাকা ডিজেল/বিদ্যুৎ বিল আসে?
   কমেন্টে লিখুন — আমরা সাশ্রয়ের হিসাব দেখাব।"
4. **Save-bait:** the Day-2 comparison infographic is designed to be *saved & shared* ("পরে কাজে লাগবে — Save করে রাখুন").
5. **WhatsApp click-to-chat** link in every post: `https://wa.me/8801722002125`.
6. **Boost the winners:** after 24h, put a small budget (see 1.6) behind the post with the best organic
   saves/shares — not the one with most likes.

### 1.6 Paid amplification (optional but recommended)
- **Total 3-day test budget:** ৳6,000–৳15,000 (~$50–$130).
- **Facebook:** Engagement + "Send Message" objective, geo = Dhaka + Chattogram + Gazipur + Narayanganj
  (industrial belts), age 28–55, interests: *manufacturing, solar energy, RMG, factory, generator, business owner*.
- **LinkedIn:** Boost Post **1B** and **3B** only; target *Owner/Director/Manager* in *Manufacturing, Textiles,
  Pharmaceuticals, Logistics* in Bangladesh.
- Put 60% behind Day-2 (solution) and 40% behind Day-3 (proof/CTA).

### 1.7 Lead capture & response (close the loop)
- Every lead → reply with the **AI chatbot** link first ("send a photo of your bill/panel/site").
- Qualify in DM with 3 questions: (1) load size / monthly bill, (2) location, (3) home or business.
- Hand qualified industrial leads to an engineer; log name + phone. Aim: **first human reply < 2 hours**.

### 1.8 KPIs to watch (not vanity)
Saves + Shares (intent), DMs / WhatsApp clicks, website link clicks, chatbot conversations started,
qualified leads, cost-per-lead (if boosted). Likes are last.

### 1.9 Governance / do-not-cross (brand safety)
- **Real numbers only.** Use catalog/website figures; never promise an exact rupee saving without a survey —
  say **"up to"** and **"after a free assessment."**
- Don't quote a final price publicly; route to consultation.
- Keep the three brand colours: **True Blue #0073CF · Lime Green #32CD32 · Golden Poppy #FCC200**.
- Bangla must read naturally (not machine-translated). Copy below is human-natural Bangla.
